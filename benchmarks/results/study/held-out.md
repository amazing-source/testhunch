# Ranking study: held-out

Generated by `python -m benchmarks.study`; method in docs/adr/0014, held-out projects (docs/adr/0013). Primary measure: APFDc with a job's failures as one fault, averaged per project over its failing jobs ranked from a non-empty history, then over the 10 RTPTorrent projects. A candidate beats the current version when the 95% bootstrap interval of the mean difference is above 0, the mean difference is at least 0.005, and it scores higher on at least 6 of 10 projects.
